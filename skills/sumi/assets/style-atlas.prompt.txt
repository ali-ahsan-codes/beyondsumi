Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE beautifully art-directed comparison board titled "SUMI — 8 VISUAL VARIATIONS". This is an educational style sheet, a single finished image, not eight separate assets. Landscape 16:9 layout, high resolution. Eight equally sized cinematic picture panels in a neat 4-column by 2-row grid on warm ivory paper, with comfortable narrow gutters. Each panel has its exact one-word label clearly typeset just below its picture; labels top row left to right "WASH", "GRAIN", "PRINT", "NOIR"; bottom row "MIST", "SKETCH", "FLOW", "SCENIC". Small elegant title above the grid; no extra explanatory text, no numbers, no logo, no watermark.
Every panel interprets the SAME simple original scene: a solitary long-necked crane bird at a shallow pond, one bending pine tree, distant mountain, drifting single leaf. Compose it consistently with the crane as main subject, pine to one side, mountain in distance. The scene should feel like a deliberate beautiful frame from a cinematic animated short, not a clip-art icon. No copyrighted character designs. The eight mediums MUST be visibly distinct:
WASH: our original digital Sumi-e approach, expressive black brush and diluted grey wet ink on offwhite fibrous Japanese paper. Bleeding organic mountain edges, delicate mist, dry brush pine, carefully articulated crane silhouette, ample negative space, only one tiny red leaf.
GRAIN: strongly designed angular graphic silhouettes, sage and near-black with cream, dense beautiful stipple shading that sculpts the forms, simplified dramatic crane gesture and layered geometric mountain planes. Contemporary painted graphic animation, not a watercolor filter.
PRINT: richly detailed ukiyo-e inspired woodblock print. Crisp fine dark contours, flat muted indigo, ochre, russet and sage colour plates, engraved feather and pine details, printed-paper texture and curled water motifs. Clearly more flat and outlined than WASH.
NOIR: cinematic hard binary black-and-ivory composition. Massive black shapes, white crane carved out by negative space, selective white feather cuts, sharp dark pine, high contrast moon. Almost no grey, one tiny vermilion leaf, bold expressive graphic composition.
MIST: soft atmospheric photographic-painterly compositing. Naturalistic crane and realistic pine fragments emerge through granulating grey-blue ink washes, fog and dissolving image edges, while the crane's face and body remain recognizable. Very spacious pale sky, a memory-like dream image. Not vector art.
SKETCH: expressive scratchy pencil-and-ink storybook drawing. Deep charcoal crane, visible loose feather strokes and contour hatching, sparse paper surroundings, very restrained warm ochre leaf accent, intimate crane facial expression. Hand-drawn stable anatomy, fewer environmental details, no glossy rendering.
FLOW: abstract calligraphy, where the same crane, pond and pine are only suggested by sweeping black ink ribbons and fine parallel dry-bristle strands, varying brush pressure, graceful flowing curves, dark pools contrasting untouched white space. Truly abstract, not a detailed bird painting.
SCENIC: lush hand-painted anime-inspired colour environment with cel-like crane. Atmospheric mountains, luminous late-afternoon sky, detailed pine foliage, turquoise pond reflections, restrained warm light, cinematic depth and beautiful scenery. Distinctly coloured and painterly, no ink wash filter.
Make the differences unmistakable, especially GRAIN vs WASH vs PRINT vs SKETCH. Detailed, sophisticated, intentional artwork. Labels must be exact and perfectly legible.
