## Why eight, not thirteen

- Original **Wash** remains intact and gains the Blade & Soul staging notes.
- Tencent's two pins describe the same film. The festival film belongs to the related **Grain** graphic-painterly family; keep its differences as options in that guide.
- Botanical, cat and Sumo references share **Print** material language. Their action/loop/environment requirements are retained as subcases, not thrown away by merging.
- **Noir**, **Mist**, **Sketch** and **Flow** have distinct contour, compositing or motion requirements; merging them merely because they use black would lose useful instructions.
- **Scenic** retains the colour-anime reference honestly as an adjacent option. It does not change Wash's default palette.

## Adoption and remaining limits

Each variation has a specific asset contract, motion route, sound direction and review criteria. Shared code probes validate several reusable mechanisms: deterministic timing, stable texture, branch attachment/release, shape division, pressure-ribbon reveal, blink/pivot, parallax and IK math. They are original schematic studies. They do not prove studio-quality character drawing, photographic asset quality, production narration, arbitrary camera orbits, real fluid dynamics, or every action in the reference films.

For a real commission, first validate the hardest representative shot with production assets. Use alternate poses, in-betweens or geometry when needed. Preserve user-approved successes, and refine the appropriate guide from observed failures. No video-generation service, copied reference frames, paid speech generation or external publication was used for this skill-writing task.
