# Connected scene and prop continuity

Use in Original, Light and Dark with the existing scene-first plan, complete-pose
preparation and acting/physics review. Design the story state before artwork;
correct framing or more output frames cannot repair an impossible sequence.

For recurring subjects/props, keep a small ledger:

| Shot / event frame | Incoming state and owner | Visible action/contact | Outgoing state / next shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Track only story-relevant details: who knows what, emotional change, injuries,
clothing, occupied/empty hands, object owner/side/orientation, open/closed or
covered/uncovered state, route, entrance/exit and eyelines. Carry consequences
across shots. A deliberate flashback or elapsed-time change must read clearly.
Do not introduce duplicate objects or repeat an event just to reuse artwork.

An invitation sequence is a useful example: empty hands → courier handoff →
open/read → put away. Do not show an opened letter before delivery, deliver it
again later or reset an opened letter to sealed. An uncovered reveal stays
uncovered unless someone visibly covers it. These are examples of state
continuity, not mandatory scenes for other stories.

For walking and connected action, choose the route, floor, facing, formation,
support and destination before drawing. Use complete, aligned sequential poses
and enough contact/weight-transfer breakdowns for the actual action. Keep body,
feet and ground travel coherent. Do not repair mismatched feet with arbitrary
limb deformation or a fake stride; reuse a valid whole pose, prepare matching
drawings, or simplify the action while preserving its meaning. A group walk
needs shared staging. Short held-foot intervals and supported local hair/cloth
motion can complement whole poses; they do not certify a complete gait.

Review each used crop/mask against its complete master, including pale clothes,
hair, shoes, hands and required props. Deliberate portraits differ from missing
limbs. Check every scene, entry/exit and consequence at normal speed in the
actual render; sample setup, contact and recovery as well as the most dramatic
frame. When a flaw is found, repair the smallest affected source/shot and its
dependent timing/sound. Preserve working scenes and previous versions.

Retain the selected clocks: Original30fps/20 designed character states per
animated second; Light/Dark24fps/paired states up to12changes/s. Distinct source
drawings, derived motion states, reused cycles, output frames and camera/weather
changes are different counts. Never report20new AI drawings/s from a20Hz driver.

Keep separate voice/music/ambience/SFX and the shared event ledger. If the story
announces that drums stop, audible drums must precede the stop; align it with the
actual picture and let the intended tail/silence read. Verify narration clarity,
material contact, measured duck/recovery, loop joins and final encoded listening.
Reuse the offline sound bank; a picture repair does not authorize a new paid
take. Positive feedback on a film is not approval of every individual bank asset.
