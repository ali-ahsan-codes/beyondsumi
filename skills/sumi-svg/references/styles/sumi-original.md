# Sumi Original in SVG

Use expressive Japanese Sumi-e painted shapes and atmospheric depth. Match all subjects/environment to that material; avoid generic flat icon people. SVG texture is a designed approximation, not a claim of painterly image-model detail. Emotional/character stories can use any story-selected palette, including dark colours.

Compose whole scenes first: paper/sky, distant wash, environment/floor/support, complete subjects/props, foreground. Use broad irregular fills, sparse dry-edge variation and negative space. Prepare supported front/side/detail views for cinematic cuts; a crop is not another angle. Keep complete sequential poses for travel/contact and selected face/eyes/hair/cloth/breath acting for quiet emotion.

30fps output/20 designed character states per animated second, using checked whole geometry or supported articulation with alternating one/two-frame exposures. These are not20new illustrations/s. Preserve planted support/grips and protect faces/rigid props. Document deliberate stillness and review actual motion.

Original uses its own Sumi Anime-inspired title and clean/mist edits, without animated brush handwriting, action slashes or splash reveals. Default full-frame16:9. Raised compact stylish backed captions explain the story even without narration. Titles last≤3seconds total with individual word entries. Only approved title/captions/necessary story text appears in the film.

Use the [shared SVG workflow](../workflow.md) for environment, sound, proof and export. A darker story does not turn Original into Dark.
