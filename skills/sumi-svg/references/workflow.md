# Scene-first SVG art and movement

Write the shot's visible action/emotion first. Choose the angle and composition before drawing: establishing geography, medium interaction or a supported detail. Budget enough space around complete character/prop extents and intended camera movement. For a real new angle draw a matching new view; do not rotate a flat body and call it a camera orbit.

Build depth deliberately: paper/sky, far mountains/buildings, middle environment, complete subjects/props, local foreground. Give meaningful groups IDs such as `clouds`, `subject`, `face`, `hair`, `left-hand`, `right-hand`, `lantern-flame`, `receiver-light`, `water` and `foreground`. Use broad irregular painted silhouettes, pooled/dry edges, restrained gradients and negative space; avoid uniform vector outlines and decorative scatter. Limit filters to bounded regions and test them in the actual browser.

Treat anatomy/contact as authored geometry. Prepare whole sequential poses for walking/reaching/fighting; preserve body proportions, handedness, prop ownership and ground line. Plan anticipation/contact/response/settle. Selective blink/hair/cloth/light motion can reuse a checked master; moving weather is not character acting. Constrain feet/hand grips and occlusion in the same coordinates as the whole source. Never assemble unrelated limb fragments.

Use one film-clock state, deterministic seeds and output-frame sampling; do not depend on an uncontrolled SMIL clock for export. Original's20 designed states/s and Light/Dark's paired states are separate from output resolution. Deliberate stillness is valid when declared. Camera only moves within supported art; titles clear within3seconds total and captions stay compact and readable.

Render at delivery aspect, inspect the full composition, close face/hands/feet and hardest action in motion. Check recognizable expression, count limbs and animal subjects, retain complete silhouettes, and verify source/target masks do not bend protected objects. One attractive static SVG does not prove action quality. Prefer simpler honest direction over deforming anatomy to imitate complex movement. Keep supplied reference videos for studying timing, never trace/copy their frames.


## Story captions and audible non-narrated sound · 26 October 2026

Owner clarification applies to **Sumi Original, Sumi Light and Sumi Dark**, including SVG artwork: retain compact readable story captions even when there is no narration or dialogue, unless the user explicitly asks for a text-free film. Write concise scene-linked captions from the approved story so the viewer understands cause, action and consequence. Spoken films still use approved speech-aligned captions. Keep faces/hands/feet and important action clear, use the style's appropriate backing, and preserve the separate three-second title rule. No sample signatures, scene labels, technical notes or other unapproved text belongs in the film.

Without narration/dialogue, music and environmental sound carry the film. Keep an attractive, clearly audible, story-shaped background score; do not leave narration ducking active across a speechless film. Layer distinct contact-aligned material effects and ambience, with purposeful build, brief contrast, impact and a resolved ending. Rich sound is not continuous maximum volume or a generic whoosh over every frame. Preserve deliberate dramatic quiet, recover the score, avoid clipping, measure the encoded mix and audition when the modality is available. Meters and unmuted browser playback do not constitute independent listening approval. Use the selected library/Python route and ask before any new paid acquisition.
