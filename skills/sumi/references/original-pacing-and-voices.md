# Original — continuous pacing and role voices

Apply to new/revised Original films alongside [Living Frames](living-frames.md). Preserve the entire approved story and natural speech rate. Do not stretch a short take to the nominal runtime by inserting long blank gaps, frozen shots or replayed reactions. Build the picture to actual alignment, retaining natural breaths. A pause needs visible story progress or a deliberate, brief emotional purpose; inspect pauses and repetitive shots at normal speed. If a runtime change has not already been requested/authorized, present the concrete timing options before cutting, rushing or extending.


Keep speech intelligible and foreground effects audible on ordinary playback: separate voice/music/ambience/SFX stems, narration ducking with natural recovery, synchronized material contacts, clear opening sound and controlled dramatic build/stop/decay. Use loudness/true-peak measurements to find technical faults, then listen; a low meter reading or a large normalization gain is not proof of emotional quality. Avoid clipping, pumping, sudden room-tone holes or music covering dialogue. Keep compact raised captions readable. Record unperformed listening and action checks honestly.

Character casting and lip-sync are shared by all styles; read [the common dialogue guide](dialogue-and-lip-sync.md) when roles speak.
