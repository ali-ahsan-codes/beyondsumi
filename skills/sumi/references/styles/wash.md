# Wash

A fluid ink-space treatment related to the original Sumo ink method: pale washes, dense brush silhouettes and physical action moving through layered ink space. Subjects, palette accents and duration come from the prompt; samurai, red scarves and previous films are examples, not defaults.

## Binding movement identity

Read [the category contract](../animation-categories.md). Wash is fluid **painted performance through space**. The line of action, silhouette, local brush contour, cloth, terrain and camera should cooperate. A finished ink character translated on a flat strip of ground is not enough. Preserve the user's liked ink/paper palette while directing darkness, scale, occlusion and energy for the current story.

The Blade & Soul reference is a vocabulary of advancing poses, airborne arcs, changing depth, rough painted terrain and direction-led edits. Do not convert it into a universal walk cycle. Start with the key visual event, draw the anticipation/contact/recovery silhouettes and required views, then connect them with deliberate in-betweens, local articulation and short authored smears. Have ink edges follow changing poses and pressure; reserve splashes for force/change of state. Movement may be continuous and sleek without making every contour perfectly digital.

**Build recipe:** separate paper, distant translucent space, traversable terrain, action poses/rig, free cloth/props, and near occluders. Block world-space performance first, map the painted poses to it, then add local edge evolution and depth-linked atmosphere. Track, descend, approach or withdraw when the action calls for it. A foreground mass may conceal a legitimate matched change of shot, but must not conceal missing contact.

**Identity proof:** play a representative passage with camera locked, then with the final camera. The subject must perform in the first and the spatial journey must add meaning in the second. Show a meaningful changing paint silhouette and a motivated transition/reveal where needed. Reject a generic walking rig, uniform sine bobbing, static brush textures pretending to be material motion, or the same stage for every beat. Print and Noir can intentionally inherit this movement family; each still directs its own shots and material behavior.

## Materials and assets

Use warm paper, several ink densities and deliberate negative space. Far forms lose contrast before structure; foreground ink can be nearly solid. Keep pigment attached to its object and paper fibres stable. A sparse accent identifies a meaningful subject or changed state. Avoid noise crawling over faces.

Prepare overscanned scenery with useful depth planes, clean action terrain, foreground occluders and hidden-area coverage. Terrain may be diagonal or uneven when the shot needs it; ground contacts must match that terrain. Subjects need separate head/limbs/cloth, independent props, anticipation/contact/recovery drawings and any changed viewpoints. Leave pivot overlap under clothing and inspect true alpha against light/dark. Make detailed close views rather than enlarging vague wide-shot faces.

Share deterministic helpers, not another variant's shot graph. Wash needs its own performance, camera trajectory, transition causes and spatial plan.

## Subject performance

Stage intention in silhouette. A weight shift prepares travel, a jump or a strike; acceleration leads the main action; follow-through and recovery reveal its cost. Distance and body scale drive stride phase. During stance the foot stays anchored in world space, then visibly lifts, swings and replants. Use IK or matched drawings with consistent volume. Full turns, foreshortening and grapples need additional artwork or geometry.

Airborne motion needs launch, trajectory, orientation and landing compression. Camera movement must keep the decisive apex/contact readable even during fast traversal. Held props follow transformed grips. Weapon motion includes torso/shoulder/arm contribution; the target reacts at actual contact. On release, preserve position and inherited velocity before gravity/drag and collision take over.

Pin cloth roots and let the free ends lag body acceleration before settling under coherent wind. A designed curve or baked constrained chain is valid. Independent sine motion applied to every body part does not create weight.

## Camera and edit rhythm

Use space dynamically: lateral accompaniment, diagonal travel, descent, changing figure scale or a near occluder revealing the next view. Select the route for the story; do not make every scene the same horizontal stage. Foreground moves according to depth and can briefly conceal an edit when the exit/entrance directions agree.

Track during a journey, lag to reveal danger, or settle before contact. A push-in exposes a prepared expression/grip; a pullback reveals a new relationship or threat. Quiet observation, anticipation, rapid action and residual settle need different phrasing. Camera motion cannot substitute for subject performance. Limit impact shake to a real event and let it decay.

A branch, moving silhouette, architecture or wash can carry a transition when its direction and geometry connect both shots. Do not repeat a generic slash between every scene. A dissolve follows a shown consequence, applies to every child and leaves the removed subject removed.

## Atmosphere and entrances

Place mist in explicit depth bands. Leading wisps and a denser body travel coherently; depth or local turbulence can explain speed differences. A character may emerge through a gap while its shadow and mask follow the same transform. Fog must not erase floor contact or hide missing anatomy.

Ink reveals have a readable solid core and lower-density edge. Newly wet edges expand slightly and settle locally; the whole image must not churn. Cache masks and seeds. Review blur/occlusion at speed so they direct attention without hiding broken poses.

## Narration, sound and captions

Provider choice follows [narration setup](../narration.md): ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or none. Style direction never overrides that choice.

Keep the complete approved narration and measure decoded timing before locking the edit. For a runtime conflict, follow [script and duration](../script-duration.md): reuse existing authorization or ask the current user before extending, shortening or rewriting. Never silently omit lines to hit a preset length.

Use the selected narration route and Python synthesis for original score, air and stylized foley. Brush/noise gestures can follow motion pressure; footsteps, cloth tension, weapon contact and settling debris need distinct events. Leave quiet around decisions, duck music beneath narration and sync captions to the actual take. The proposed sound is not a reconstruction of unheard reference audio.

Keep stable phrase captions in clear space, away from faces, hands and contact. Typography may fit the paper without turning every sentence into a title card.

## Representative preflight and failure cases

Select proofs for the mechanics actually required by the current brief. Do not add a character, fight, camera move or transformation merely to satisfy an example below; a simple nature shot may need only its own release/contact and material proof. Explicit requests for silence, no narration or no captions override the defaults above.

Before the full film, prove the hardest Wash motion with production assets: required traversal/occlusion and a local action with contact/recovery. Review normal speed and consecutive frames around support, release, apex and landing. Inspect maximum crop, blur, bleed edges and depth ordering.

Fail for sliding feet, floating grips, repeated stamp halos, detached cloth, contact hidden by shake, a whole painting translated as one layer or a palette-swapped common performance. The branch-release lab validates a bounded mechanism, not a finished fight. Apply [motion direction](../motion-direction.md), [physics and review](../physics-and-review.md), [motion recipes](../motion-recipes.md) and the [production standard](../production-standard.md).

**Adaptable example:** a messenger traverses a windy pass, disappears behind a near rock and reappears lower in the ravine; a close decision beat leads into a visibly grounded rescue. Choose a different construction for another story. No journey, fight, shot order or timing is mandatory.
