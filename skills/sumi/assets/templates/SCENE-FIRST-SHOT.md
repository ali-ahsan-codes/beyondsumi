Artwork decision: intact contextual panel or derived layers; every derived layer's parent master/hash, crop/registration and occlusion comparison. Required new matching views and reason a crop cannot supply them:

Asset strategy: compatible exact reuse → supported local transform/rig → derive master → pose sheet → new composition. List existing candidates, actual selection/rejection reasons, new-call budget, requested panel resolution and inspected usable regions:

Animation strategy: `whole_pose`, `living_frame` or `mixed`. For a living frame, record the emotional focus, one/two primary details, up to two supportive motions, optional scene-specific atmosphere, motivated camera/stillness, scalp/cloth anchors, protected face/grip/support regions, original master/mask/clean-plate hashes, motion start/settle, and seam/occlusion review. Do not regenerate the entire scene to move hair.

Acting record (`acting`): actor IDs; knowledge/feeling/concealment; primary visible change and anticipation/onset/peak/response/settle frames; supported eyes/face/hands; secondary motion; contact/support intervals; deliberate stillness; per-source runtime bindings/hashes; actual preview/export observations and unresolved defects. Check every exposed source, including endings; keep review pending until performed.

Original:30fps/20 designed character states per animated second, alternating one/two-frame exposures; action-driven new-source budget and supported derived states. Document deliberate stillness separately. Light/Dark:24fps, paired character states and longer even holds. Camera/environment use the output clock. Light: explain story emotion and cause/effect, subject/object emphasis and story-selected washes. Dark: explain subject importance, larger brush/splash forms, face/detail/angle and camera-led reveal, neutral base/selective dark red and quiet reading intervals. Original: preserve Sumi-e/Sumi Anime art, emotional acting and clean/mist continuity without animated splash/brush reveals.

Sound: actual contact frames; separate voice/music/ambience/SFX; duck/attack/release/recovery; dramatic buildup/stop/decay and listening status:

Proof clip/hash; actual adjacent frames and normal-speed observations; unresolved flaws; smallest affected repair; next batch decision:

Never fill passing art/physics/listening status before review.

Environmental direction: animate/still decision and reason; local sources/receivers, weather/wind/depth, source-specific masks, protected characters/architecture/text and source-linked sound. Complete [the environment shot record](ENVIRONMENT-SHOT.md); review actual sequences, not only parameters.

Cross-shot state: prop ID/owner/state/side; character knowledge/injury/clothing; route/facing; incoming/outgoing state and intentional elapsed-time changes. Follow [continuity](../../references/production-continuity.md); do not reset a completed action.

Sound proof: audible lead-in/build before a named stop; source/contact frame; speech intelligibility; duck/recovery and tails; loop joins; actual encoded listening observations/pending items.

Delivery proof: actual picture at mid-clip and near ending, normal-speed controls/audio, downloaded hash and206range response. Use [playback review](../../references/review-playback.md); mark pending checks honestly.

Title record (all three styles): maximum3seconds from first visible title/backing to fully gone, entrance+stable read+exit included. Words enter individually in reading order; record exact title/unit timings in `title_timing.cards`. Use Original’s clean Sumi Anime motion, Light’s restrained brush reveals or Dark’s story-led splash/horror treatment. Ordinary narration/moral captions retain reading time. Check actual exported entrance/full-title/title-free frames; do not extend the film for a bumper.
