# Story-led animation production standard

Production rules for this standalone Sumi skill. The style changes the materials and motion vocabulary; the story determines the shots. These are working production requirements, not claims about traditional painting or official Vox practice. Updated 20 October 2026.

## Scope and defaults

Use this when creating or revising a Sumi film. A request to document/research a method does not start paid generation or a new film. Honor the current brief over defaults. Follow the chosen narration provider or no-narration path in [narration.md](narration.md), with requested background sound and readable captions when relevant. Obtain approval of the concrete direction plan under [director-workflow.md](director-workflow.md) before dependent production. Use the user's selected browser for playback reviews. Build motion with images plus code, SVG, Canvas or another suitable scripted renderer; external AI video generation is outside these methods unless explicitly requested.

Do not reuse the samurai plot, English language, stock voice, 29/60-second timing or 16:9 format as universal requirements. Read the actual topic, tone, audience, language, duration and destination. Inherit an existing project's format; when unspecified for a new film, state a reasonable assumption and proceed. Preserve the ability to revise and rerender.

## Learn methods; adapt to each prompt

Treat each style as a learned visual vocabulary, not a locked preset. Study what a reference's layers, materials, motion, composition and sound accomplish, then choose how to achieve the current story. A reference's plot, duration, palette, typography, camera, tools and shot order are examples, not compulsory defaults. Reuse principles rather than clone compositions.

Start each film with a short treatment tied to its prompt: audience takeaway/emotion, primary materials, palette roles, typography, motion, sound and the reason for any map or dimensional view. Extend or combine techniques when justified while maintaining coherence. The current explicit request can change prior defaults, including caption-only text, visual density, narration, aspect ratio or pacing. Preserve physical/spatial credibility and evidence accuracy regardless of treatment.

For each new style or useful reference, record directly observed qualities, source-reported methods, our proposed adaptation and unresolved limits separately. Keep the methods distinct for discovery, but permit intentional hybrids. Do not force a samurai fight or one reference plot onto every story.

## Script preservation and runtime conflicts

Follow [script-duration.md](script-duration.md) for all users. Approved spoken words, required visible text and essential visual actions are separate deliverables; preserve each. Estimate the full timeline before paid media. A fixed target is not permission to delete content. If the complete cut does not fit, show its likely length and a concrete proposed shorter edit, then ask which the user wants. Reuse explicit permission already given to extend or shorten. Measure the actual take and revisit unresolved timing conflicts; do not truncate or speed through the story.

## Directed animation in the three styles

Follow [the final three-style contract](animation-categories.md), [complete-character integrity](character-integrity.md), [physical review](physics-and-review.md) and the selected [style-profile schema](style-profile-schema.md). Original preserves the first Sumi-e movement identity; Light/Dark share technical preparation, with distinct Dark splash-built forms and camera-led direction. All require actual performed action, appropriate camera, atmosphere and consequence; palette alone cannot supply a missing action.

Save complete designs and poses first. Once testing/production is authorized, prove the hardest contact and material passage before full rendering. Inspect adjacent frames and normal-speed playback. Use a monochrome fixed-camera diagnostic when useful, without demanding different engines from Light/Dark. Current proof records/hashes support the gate; metadata does not establish artistic truth. Documentation-only work and a user instruction to defer testing must be respected.

## Whole-film consistency

Maintain a scene coverage ledger using [action preparation](action-pose-sheets.md). Check every required performance and object event, including the opening, commands, animals and quiet scenes, rather than accepting a film because its hardest fight looks good. Compare representative frames across the full film for shared material, character identity and drawing detail. Light/Dark share source preparation; Dark has a distinct oversized splash-built character/world design and stronger camera-led motion with dark red. Original uses contextual preparation with action-driven drawing budgets and20 designed character states/s at30fps. An intentional still aftermath is valid; a frozen commanding gesture with camera movement is not a substitute for the required gesture.

## Story before asset count

Write a short causal spine: situation → change/problem → consequential action or explanation → payoff. Fiction can use a failed attempt and transformation; a factual explainer needs a defensible question, mechanism and answer. Neither requires the same formula in every story.

Make a shot plan with these fields:

| Field | What to decide |
|---|---|
| Beat and takeaway | What the viewer must understand or feel |
| Narration phrase | What is said; what can be left to the image |
| Visible change | A concrete action, relationship or consequence |
| Start/end state | Where the subject, props and outcome are before/after |
| Assets and motion | Separate parts, required poses, pivots and interaction |
| Camera | Framing, trajectory, focal subject and continuity |
| Contact/event | Exact event shared by image, reaction and sound |
| Caption space | Readable placement that avoids the gesture |
| Review moments | Setup, anticipation, contact, aftermath, cut boundaries |

Before polishing, ask whether the main visual sequence still communicates the core action without narration. An abstract factual topic may need labels; a fight cannot rely on the narrator saying a strike occurred when the image never shows contact. End with a visible consequence or resolved relationship, with time to register it.

## More graphics, with a purpose

Build rich story-relevant graphics and expressive animation without filling the frame with unnecessary words. Add detail through better environments, expressive poses, foreground/midground/background separation, animated props, diagrams where relevant, controlled light and atmosphere, and clear changes of state. Introduce detail progressively. One dominant action must remain easy to follow.

Do not use a flat poster as every shot. Prepare independent subjects and props, clean backgrounds, alternate views/poses and close-up detail assets. A generated still is a source asset, not the animation. Changing poses without overlap/continuity can pop; a single rigid pose moved across the frame cannot convincingly perform a multi-stage action. Choose layered articulation, deliberately matched pose transitions or an appropriate drawn/vector rig for the shot.

For action-critical assets, inspect silhouette, anatomy, hands, costume, weapon length, face identity and screen direction before generation proliferates. Design the contact pose first, then anticipation and follow-through. If a sword must leave a hand and be planted, make it an independent prop and provide an empty-hand pose. Avoid a baked-in duplicate sword or scarf. Request genuine alpha for independent cutouts and inspect edges against both light and dark backgrounds.

Plan resolution for the largest crop/zoom in the final frame. Inspect assets at delivery size; an upscale or high-resolution container cannot repair low-detail input. Keep object textures stable in object/world space; explicitly separate deliberate screen-space grain and evolving wet pigment from those fixed layers; these are internal techniques, not extra style choices. Separate factual evidence from illustration and retain provenance.

## Text and captions

Use a prominent, readable animated opening/topic title and a considered ending under [hooks, endings and sound](hooks-endings-sound.md). Keep continuing narration captions compact. Avoid large repeated narration headlines, paragraphs and webpage-like sections. Do not duplicate the spoken line as both a caption and a graphic headline. Use brief editorial labels only when they explain something captions cannot efficiently identify, such as a place, date, axis or comparison. A requested kinetic-typography treatment is a deliberate exception.

Use a readable caption face suited to the selected style, short phrase groups and clear margins. Light/Dark prefer brief brush reveals and stable text in negative space; use a quiet backing only where contrast needs it. Keep captions away from faces, hands and impact points. Let a phrase hold long enough to read; avoid attention-stealing per-letter motion by default. For Urdu, use a font with shaping support and correct direction, preserve complete words and punctuation, and review the rendered line. Don't substitute Roman Urdu unless requested.

A browser preview should emphasize the frame: minimal overlay controls, auto-hide during playback, accessible Play/Pause/Replay, captions, sound, seeking and fullscreen where supported. The exported video excludes player controls and authoring/debug UI.

## Physical credibility

Stylized physics must preserve cause and effect. Use directed keyframes where clearer; a simulation is not a quality badge. Decide whether the object behaves as paper, a body, cloth, fluid or a graphic symbol, and keep that behavior consistent.

- **Locomotion:** stride phase follows distance. During stance, a foot stays fixed on the ground while the body passes over it; lift, swing and replant deliberately. Use IK or designed poses rather than sliding a rigid standing figure and calling it walking.
- **Weight:** show anticipation, acceleration, contact, follow-through and settling where the action needs them. Larger masses normally resist acceleration and settle differently from light paper. Do not apply identical spring bounces to everything.
- **Ballistics:** in screen coordinates with y downward, use `p(t)=p0+v0*t+0.5*g*t*t` for free flight. Switch to contact behavior at the landing; don't continue through the floor. Rotation belongs around a plausible center of mass or physical joint.
- **Contact:** define a world-space anchor and a contact time. Blade/hand/object, target deformation or reaction, particles and the effect onset use this same event. At the selected output rate (Original 30fps, Light/Dark 24fps), inspect adjacent frames to avoid a multi-frame reaction before impact; deliberate stylization must still read correctly.
- **Collisions:** use the silhouette's relevant extremities or colliders, not only its center. Check heads, horns, weapons and rotated limbs at the ground and frame edges. Keep a planted object planted after contact.
- **Attachments:** hands keep their grip; scarves stay attached to the correct body; strings connect to transformed anchors; tree ties wrap where the sword and trunk actually meet. Track prop ownership across cuts.
- **Cloth:** use separate chains, pinned roots, length constraints, damping and one consistent wind/gravity frame. Bake fixed-step simulation for arbitrary-time rendering; a fixed time step alone does not guarantee reproducible seeks.
- **Outcomes:** a broken, defeated or removed object stays in that state. Stop idle movement after death, settle remains, and propagate opacity to all parts before a dissolve. Do not revive it accidentally on a later shot.
- **Camera:** keep crucial contacts and jump apexes in frame. Use limited, decaying shake tied to a real impact. Camera movement cannot substitute for subject action.

For new projects, run the [portable physics gate](physics-gate.md) before final rendering; do not bypass it with a direct render command. For character shots, complete the [limb-motion review](limb-motion-review.md). Inspect full-body action transitions and visible soles, palms and prop grips; a successful hardest-shot proof cannot cover unreviewed movements.

For physical events, record the intended constraint and inspect both normal-speed motion and closely spaced frames. Numeric checks support visual review; they do not prove that a gesture feels convincing.

## Narration and soundtrack

Follow [narration.md](narration.md) for ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or no narration. Read saved preferences first. A voice selected for one project is not permission to change persistent defaults. Discover actual voice/model and language support; do not freeze model names, pricing or settings into these methods. A blocked cloned voice is not permission to substitute a different voice without authorization.

Write the complete script and performance direction before paid generation. Generate only within the requested production scope, preserve receipt/request ID, script, voice/model and alignment, and inspect any uncertain result before retrying. Never place API credentials in a project, skill or browser bundle.

Measure decoded audio duration and use its phrase timing to finalize the timeline. Keep space for action, breaths and the ending. Do not force fit by speeding up every scene or speech take. Create captions from the actual take; a changed take invalidates affected cues.

Complete the [required scene sound coverage](hooks-endings-sound.md#sound-coverage-is-required). Check each named sound against the actual mix, including audible drums before a planned stop; a missing or masked story cue needs repair. Maintain voice, music, effects and optional ambience as separate stems while editing. Music should follow tension/release and duck beneath narration. Use distinct effects for consequential contact and aftermath; a whoosh alone does not communicate a hit. Match sound mass/material to the image, and leave intentional silence. The ElevenLabs Voice plugin does not imply music or effects generation capabilities; use suitable authorized assets or locally synthesized sound and describe provenance accurately.

Check the complete encoded mix for silence/truncation, intelligibility, sync, loudness and clipping. Pick a target appropriate to the destination; roughly −16 LUFS integrated and at most −1 dBTP is a useful web starting point, not a mandatory platform standard. Technical meters cannot validate pronunciation, emotional delivery or taste. If no listening modality is available, mark listening as unverified; do not call the mix fully reviewed.

## Deterministic implementation

Use one master clock for animation, captions, camera and audio. Render state from absolute time/frame with seeded variation. Precompute stateful simulation from a known initial state or store deterministic checkpoints, so playback, reverse seeking and exported frames agree. A history-dependent cloth warm-up is a prototype compromise, not a reusable export solution.

Preload images, fonts and decoded audio before starting. Use one fixed design coordinate system and scale the whole composition to the viewport. Cancel stale sound schedules on Pause, Seek and Replay; prevent overlapping playback epochs. Hold the final frame and audio tail. Follow the chosen engine's installed/current documentation for APIs instead of prescribing unnecessary packages.

Keep script, treatment, shot plan, timeline, source, selected assets/provenance, audio stems, captions, export and review evidence together in the project. Do not generate media inside an installed skill. Keep existing work intact when making a style variant.

## Review gate before publishing or calling a film final

A new style guide is not itself a finished film. Apply the following review to each actual film and the final revision, not just its earliest draft:

1. **Story:** compare the final film with the selected script and required beat ledger, including every required visible action; then watch/read once for the causal spine and ending. Can the viewer identify the action, change and outcome? Fact-check claims/maps/data when present.
2. **Graphics:** inspect the start, each shot's representative frame, close-up crops, transitions and ending at intended size. Check alpha edges, consistent anatomy, typography, layer order, occlusion and background duplicates.
3. **Motion/physics:** verify that the chosen style has its own directed motion language, then play at normal speed and inspect consecutive frames around contacts, landings, pose changes and prop handoffs. Check constraints above; confirm effects and camera support rather than conceal broken choreography.
4. **Audio/captions:** review the actual take and full mix, match key events, confirm caption wording/readability, inspect final audio tail and meter the encoded result. Record any unperformed listening check honestly.
5. **Runtime:** for localhost, open in Chrome and play from start through the real ending. Test pause/resume, replay, seek, mute/captions and fullscreen if supplied. Inspect errors and missing assets. Background-tab throttling is not a successful playback test.
6. **Export:** inspect the actual exported file's dimensions, frame rate/count, decoded duration and audio streams; watch the final export when available. A contact sheet, successful encoding or browser run alone cannot certify the other.
7. **Regression:** after fixes, revisit changed shots and affected sound, then do a final whole-film playback if timing/audio/source changed. Regenerate the export and review evidence so they correspond to the same revision.

Save a concise `REVIEW.md` or structured equivalent: revision/file identifier; performed checks and evidence; observed defects and fixes; remaining limitations; status **ready**, **draft** or **blocked**. Do not mark ready while required visual/audio review is unperformed or a material defect remains. A draft may still be shared locally with its limitations stated. Never claim perfect physics or professional quality solely from a checklist.

“Check before publishing” is a quality requirement, not authorization to upload. Complete local review and corrections; publish only to a destination the user has authorized. No additional approval gate is needed for already authorized local work.

Apply [cross-shot continuity](production-continuity.md) during scene planning and review, and [visible playback checks](review-playback.md) at delivery. A hash, source sheet, meter or advancing timer is bounded technical evidence; inspect the actual action and listen to the actual encoded mix.

## Viewer handoff

After the review above, package the exact MP4 in the [Beyondsumi dashboard](delivery-dashboard.md), with a matching review status and direct download. Default to Full HD H.264/yuv420p MP4 and AAC sound (or intentional silence); maintain the approved aspect ratio, duration and selected30fps Original or24fps Light/Dark profile. Do not substitute a browser animation or transparent transition asset for the finished MP4. Test seek, style selection and download bytes; disclose local URL lifetime.
