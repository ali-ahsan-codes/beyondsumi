# Sumi Dark in SVG

For horror/mystery/dark stories, use broad splash-built subject masses, oversized expressive brush shapes and strong readable negative space. Neutral black/grey with purposeful dark red on selected eyes, costume, blood/impact or a story motif. Red is meaningful, not a compulsory global tint. Match characters and environment; no sketch cutout placed on an unrelated ink background.

Plan stronger camera-led staging first: low wide threat, face/detail attention, occluding foreground reveal, supported push or pullback and a calm consequential ending. Draw matching alternate views; do not rotate a flat figure into a claimed orbit. Darkness must still reveal the character, decisive contact and consequence.

24fps output, paired character states up to12changes/s with justified holds. Prepare complete whole-body/interaction poses, preserve grounded support and attached hands/props, and synchronize anticipation/lunge/strike/reaction. Faces, hair and breath can carry a quiet beat; splashes/camera cannot replace absent acting.

Use larger movement-linked ink masses, splash bursts and organic local-to-scene reveals with reading intervals. A rapid lateral slide is not an ink splash. Keep eyes, grips, feet and captions clear, with contact-aligned material sound and motivated impact camera response. Horror gets its own unsettling opening scene and sound.

Retain compact backed story captions even without narration and a≤3second individually entering title. No sample signatures or technical text in the film. Use [shared SVG sound/proof guidance](../workflow.md); this guide does not claim all actions are automatically physically correct.
