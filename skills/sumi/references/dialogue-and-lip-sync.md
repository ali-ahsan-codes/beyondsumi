# Character dialogue and lip-sync · all three styles

Distinguish **offscreen narrator prose** from **words spoken by a character** before voice generation or artwork. Map each speaking role, exact approved words, language, emotional delivery and source. Use `assets/templates/VOICE-MAP.json` in the project. If no character speaks, skip this setup entirely.

For ElevenLabs/Voicebox, cast direct dialogue to the character rather than leaving every line in the narrator's performance. Reuse suitable approved voices and complete takes; request permission for paid new takes/substitutions with the exact text/model/estimate. Use only controls supported by the selected engine/endpoint. Do not send dialogue tags blindly to a model that reads them aloud. Preserve narrator wording and separately approved role lines without doubling them.

For supplied recordings, identify file→speaker→exact line, or speaker/time ranges in a combined recording. Understand and reconcile real content/timing with the host's available audio/transcription capability. Ask for unclear mapping only. Do not replace recordings with TTS, clone a voice, change emotion, cut speech or time-stretch without authorization. Keep the original; a transcript alone does not prove pronunciation or audio quality.

Use final audio timing to prepare compatible closed/rest, open, rounded and relevant articulated mouth shapes on a checked face. Keep identity, head angle and jaw attachment stable; preserve eyes/cheeks/hair and coordinate expression/breath/hand performance with the line. Align visible-speaker mouth changes to real phoneme/word timing where available; label approximate alignment when only word timing exists. Close at pauses and after the line; do not loop arbitrary mouth flaps. Offscreen narration or a back-facing character needs no visible lip-sync. Compose appropriate visible dialogue views when the approved scene needs them.

Children's roles: propose a suitable available adult performer when needed, disclose the actual performer and include casting in the approval. A female voice is an option, not a promise that every female voice sounds like a child. Offer user-recorded dialogue or supported presets/profiles. ElevenLabs' [Voice Library rules](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-library) prohibit adding child or child-like voices, including adult imitations, to that public library; do not bypass this or claim every account can create one. [Text to Dialogue](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-dialogue) supports per-turn voices for supported models, not automatic visual animation.

For Voicebox follow the installed version and its official [preset](https://docs.voicebox.sh/overview/preset-voices), [cloning](https://docs.voicebox.sh/overview/voice-cloning) and [model-management](https://docs.voicebox.sh/developer/model-management) guidance. Preset catalogues cannot be arbitrarily extended. VoiceDesign, cloning, languages and delivery controls differ by engine/version; offer only actual supported routes, with required model download consent and permission for reference voices. Do not invent a universal child-voice button or universal expressive tags.

Inspect the actual export through line start/pause/end and neighboring cuts. Check visible speaker, mouth timing, expression, complete words and voice/music/SFX balance. Keep provider output and raw human files private; the film carries only approved captions/dialogue, never performance tags or technical labels.
