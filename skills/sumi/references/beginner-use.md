# Make your first film, one step at a time

You do not need to know animation settings. Your assistant handles direction and explains the decisions in plain language. Start after [installation preparation](install/shared.md).

## 1. Start Beyondsumi

Copy this into a fresh chat:

> Use the sumi skill. I am a beginner. Read its SKILL.md, check what this computer and assistant can do, and guide me one step at a time. Do not generate a film, buy API usage or download models during setup.

You should see a short welcome and a clear report of available/missing tools. If you get only a generic answer, give the assistant the exact path to `sumi/SKILL.md` and ask it to read that file.

## 2. Choose art, voice and sound

- Art: use an available image tool for painted detail, your supplied art, or choose SVG. You can ask for one image comparing the three styles.
- Voice: ElevenLabs, free local Voicebox after an approved model download, your recorded narration, or none. Story language uses your assistant; spoken languages depend on the selected voice model.
- Sound: choose the Sumi library or fresh Python sound. Music/ambience/SFX remain available without narration; effects and mood follow the story.

The assistant should explain any missing item before installation or paid generation. You do not have to connect every option.

## 3. Give your idea or story

For example:

> Help me write a 30-second story about a lonely puppy finding a friend. I want warm narration. Show me the story before making anything.

If length or telling mode is missing, the assistant asks how long, then narrator/dialogue/both. It helps with the hook, feeling, engaging events and ending. You can supply a complete story instead and ask it to keep your wording.

## 4. Review the script and direction

Read the full words first. Ask for changes in plain language. Then review scenes, estimated duration, recommended style, character voices and sound approach. If the complete story needs longer, choose the full version or a concrete short version. Production waits for your approval of that specific plan.

## 5. Approve and review the proof

Say “Approve this script and direction; create the proof.” Only an explicitly approved paid take or download may run. The assistant checks a representative animated passage before the whole batch. Tell it what feels wrong: missing emotion, a confusing action, weak sound or an incorrect limb. It should inspect before replacing more art.

## 6. Receive and use the film

You receive an HD MP4 and Beyondsumi's branded watch/download dashboard, plus editable source and review notes. Watch the actual video with sound, then use Download MP4. A local dashboard link works while its local server runs. The dashboard shows all three styles; “Make this edition” prepares a request for your chosen agent and does not secretly generate another film.

For changes say what and where, such as “At the gate, the drums are too quiet before they stop.” The assistant reuses suitable work and fixes affected areas. Keep the supplied original recording and prior export. Story captions remain visible even without narration unless you request a text-free film.
