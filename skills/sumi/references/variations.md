# Choose one of three Sumi styles

Recommend from the story and briefly name **Sumi Original, Sumi Light and Sumi Dark**. Respect the user's choice. These are the final three; “Sumi Colo” was an interim name superseded by Sumi Light.

![Sumi Original, Sumi Light and Sumi Dark](../assets/style-atlas-three.svg)

The atlas is an original schematic teaching image, not motion proof or a screenshot of a completed film.

- Choose **Original** when the user wants the first Sumi-e film's painted world and weighted, articulated cinematic action. Read [its preserved method](styles/sumi-original.md).
- Choose **Light** when selected colours should carry meaning inside a brush-painted world. Read [Light](styles/sumi-light.md).
- Choose **Dark** when black, white and grey can carry the story through silhouette, value, negative space and expressive ink. Read [Dark](styles/sumi-dark.md).

Light/Dark share [the technical workflow](brush-ink-workflow.md), while every story receives its own direction. Original's story, sampler runtime and narrator are not fixed defaults. Use [the catalogue contract](animation-categories.md), [images first](image-first-workflow.md) and [character integrity](character-integrity.md).

Reuse the older methods as internal techniques where useful. Preserved guides and labs do not add styles and are not automatically current tested examples.
