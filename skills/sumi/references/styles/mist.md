# Mist

Recognizable painterly or photographic subjects inside an evolving ink-wash world. Its animation includes layered travel, dissolving boundaries, dramatic scale relationships and designed transitions. It is not merely fog placed over a static landscape.

## Binding fog and movement identity

Read [the category contract](../animation-categories.md). Mist is a **dense, soft-focus world with action taking place behind substantial moving fog**. Visibility itself is directed: a distant fragment, a larger silhouette, a brief readable gesture, then renewed concealment. The user's dense-fog instruction is a production choice; not every moment of the Monster source has the same opacity. Preserve its layered emergence and scale transitions without reducing Mist to a thin decorative haze.

**Build recipe:** maintain at least near/middle/far atmosphere where the shot has those depths. Give banks distinct scale, density, drift and slow local deformation under one coherent wind. Composite far subjects before nearer fog. Blend each depth toward its fog colour as transmission decreases; use focus softness separately from fog opacity. An offscreen bank can arrive fringe-first, cover the middle space, open a moving gap and leave trailing wisps. Avoid repeated horizontal ribbons or one uniform overlay. Perspective and occlusion should establish where the performer is behind it.

Blur and soften the scene deliberately; do not pin every face to a permanently sharp cutout hole. Plan **recognition windows** where the essential gesture, expression or result is visible long enough to understand. A silhouette and sound may foreshadow action; the required causal event still needs a readable reveal or an explicitly approved implication. The world need not become clear weather for every contact. For diagnosis, turn fog off and verify the underlying support/grip/geometry, then restore the intended density and review the audience's understanding.

**Identity proof:** a subject moves through actual fog depths, with coherent scale and changing occlusion, then a meaningful action/consequence becomes readable. Review darkness/brightness, softness, intermediate masks and final encoded gradients. Reject crisp characters permanently pasted above fog, weak haze, globally blurred still art, identical drift speeds, and fog used to conceal unfinished acting. Dense fog is part of the result, not a defect to remove in order to pass a generic clarity checklist.

## Materials and asset contract

Preserve recognizable detail inside a face, object or gesture. Peripheral regions may fragment into granulation, smoke or washed edges. Connect source assets with a story-specific restrained grade, giving depth layers different contrast and edge sharpness. White space actively expresses scale and isolation; it must not be an excuse for missing scenery.

Prepare a clean background, isolated subjects, interior-detail/recognition masks, separate edge fields, foreground wash and reflection/shadow layers where needed. Plan largest crop and asset resolution before generation. Photographic-looking illustration is not documentary evidence; retain original asset provenance.

Subtle breathing or a hand gesture can use local articulation, but a new expression, whole-body turn or reaching view needs extra poses/geometry. A scale transformation needs authored intermediate states or a purposeful occlusion. One still image cannot provide arbitrary acting.

Share deterministic compositing helpers, not another variation's complete shots, choreography and camera. Mist requires its own spatial transformation plan.

## Subject performance and scale

Animate an intentional gesture independently of its surrounding atmosphere. Eyes, hands and expressions become readable during planned recognition windows; they need not stay permanently sharp or above the fog. Let body/shadow/mask share a transform while preserving their physical relationship. A giant reaching figure can express a scale relationship, but the hand's actual movement and direction must read.

Design scale transitions with a meaningful anchor: a spiral's centre, a shoreline, a figure or a dominant contour can survive while the world changes. When shapes are compatible, author path correspondence. When they are not, create drawn intermediates or let a motivated field temporarily occlude the change. Midpoint inspection must show a coherent stage, not two unrelated faces or horizons superimposed.

A documentary-like detail or human core cannot dissolve before the viewer understands its action. A rescue, blow or decision still requires a visible causal event; atmospheric abstraction cannot replace it.

## Camera, space and edit rhythm

Use lateral layered travel to discover subjects inside the landscape, a measured approach to expose a gesture, or a pullback that reveals a different scale relationship. Not every shot should drift slowly. A vortex or major reveal may accelerate, resolve and then allow a quieter recognition beat. Choose cadence from the story and complete narration.

Build explicit depth: distant wash, middle landscape/figures and near haze move with coherent parallax. Do not uniformly zoom a finished composite and call it depth. Larger viewpoint changes require new plates or geometry; match direction, horizon and useful anchors across edits.

A dark shoreline may become a new horizon; a spiral may organize an arriving form; white wash may clear space for the next subject. These are optional mechanisms with narrative purpose, not transitions to repeat on a timer. A long dissolve needs continuity, not just softness.

## Atmosphere and entrances

Define fog's depth, flow direction, density and entry/exit. The pale fringe can arrive before the denser reveal and then settle. Let foreground fog cross the focal core when concealment serves the beat, and open a deliberate recognition window before the required action or result would otherwise be lost. Use seeded local fields and bounded expansion rather than frame-random turbulence.

Composite in a planned order: paper, distant wash, landscape, subject, local edge mist and foreground. Avoid multiplying faces into mud. Parent reveal masks to moving local subjects where needed; a detached screen-space mask can accidentally amputate a gesture. Keep water/reflections in their own plane rather than distorting the entire frame.

## Narration, sound and captions

Provider choice follows [narration setup](../narration.md): ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or none. Style direction never overrides that choice.

Preserve every approved line and causal beat. Measure the complete take before deciding whether a reveal has enough time to register. Follow [script and duration](../script-duration.md) for conflicts: reuse existing authorization or ask the current user before changing length/script; never silently remove narration.

Use the selected narration route and Python synthesis for original tonal development, filtered air, local detail and transformation accents. Sound can shift apparent distance as a subject emerges or changes scale. Leave space around speech; a constant airy wash must not mask consonants. This is proposed sound direction, not a claim about the source soundtrack.

Captions remain legible and stable inside protected contrast. Do not dissolve their letters in mist or add credit-style blocks unless the brief requests titles. Keep the atmospheric focal gesture distinct from the caption region.

## Representative preflight and failure cases

Select proofs for the mechanics actually required by the current brief. Do not add a character, fight, camera move or transformation merely to satisfy an example below; a simple nature shot may need only its own release/contact and material proof. Explicit requests for silence, no narration or no captions override the defaults above.

Prove the hardest required local gesture and scale/reveal transition with production assets. Inspect several progress states, midpoint, completion, largest crop and normal-speed playback. Verify detail during planned recognition windows, restored fog density, mask alignment, depth order and meaning of the resulting composition.

Fail for halos, grey alpha boxes, double faces, detached masks, uniform-image zooms, fog concealing unverified/missing anatomy throughout the causal event, or the same rig/edit as another style. The schematic mask lab does not certify a finished photographic composite. Apply [motion direction](../motion-direction.md), [physics and review](../physics-and-review.md), [motion recipes](../motion-recipes.md) and the [production standard](../production-standard.md).

**Adaptable example:** a memory of a parent emerges within a river landscape; a traced circular gesture expands into a vast protective presence, then resolves around the present-day child. Use this only when the story calls for memory/scale; Mist need not be a dream, title sequence or parent-child film.
