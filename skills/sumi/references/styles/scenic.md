# Scenic

An adjacent painted-anime variation: expressive character drawings, detailed environments, purposeful editing, and integrated 2D/3D staging when the required views need it. It can include quiet place-based observation and rhythmic montage. It is not traditional Sumi-e and must not be reduced to slow parallax over a landscape still.

## Binding anime and dimensional identity

Read [the category contract](../animation-categories.md). Scenic is the advanced painted-anime route, supporting **2D character animation with 3D space, geometry or rigs** when the shot benefits from changing perspective. The supplied compilation shows cel acting and painted worlds; a verified 3D production pipeline is not established by that reference. The 2D/3D mix is the user's requested direction for this method.

**Build recipe:** choose dimensional needs shot by shot. A subtle layered move can use 2.5D painted planes with honest depth limits. A camera orbit, traversed staircase, foreshortened weapon or changing terrain view needs actual geometry/rigging or authored view drawings. Project original painted textures onto simple scene meshes; use restrained cel/ramp shading and consistent light. Combine drawn faces/pose replacements with these spaces only where perspective, contact, scale and occlusion remain coherent. Avoid glossy plastic materials or a flat billboard that turns edge-on during an alleged 3D character turn.

Expressive acting remains primary: eye intention, changing face views, hand decisions, body weight, hair/cloth lag and visible consequence. Set camera movement in the same world as the contacts; match ground plane, light/shadow and perspective across drawings and geometry. A dolly changes spatial relationships; scaling an image is a zoom and does not reveal hidden sides. Use meaningful wide/close/reverse views instead of one perpetual side-track.

**Identity proof:** use the most demanding required face/body view change and dimensional camera move, with final art. Inspect intermediate identity, foreshortening, contour thickness, planted feet, grips, occlusion and painted lighting. If a shot uses only layered planes, call it 2.5D; if 3D is part of the promised treatment, identify and show the actual dimensional element. A static landscape with parallax alone cannot prove this category's acting or a 3D claim.

## Art and asset contract

Create a coherent painted world with defined light direction, atmospheric depth and compatible character contours. Palette comes from the story: warm sky/cool terrain or restrained night blue are options. Foreground framing must not hide the decisive gesture. Avoid generic saturated gradients and unrelated decorative particles.

Prepare clean sky/distant land, middle terrain/architecture, near ground and foreground foliage with overscan and revealed-area coverage. Water, reflections, light and clouds need separate masks/coordinate frames. For close-ups, prepare eyes, face angles, expressions, mouth/head views and detailed hands at the intended crop. A camera rotation on one portrait is not a new facial performance.

Purposeful body action needs full key poses and in-betweens or a rig capable of the required projection. New perspective/foreshortening requires new drawings or actual geometry. Share technical infrastructure, but author Scenic's own shots, acting, camera and edit rhythm.

## Character performance

Direct what the character notices, decides and does. A dramatic eye close-up must convey a change through gaze, eyelid, brow or a prepared expression, not just a moving camera. A rotated/turning face needs consistent anatomy and identity through views. Match the outgoing and incoming expression across a cut.

Walking uses world-space support and a complete gait. A seated figure can breathe, glance or move a hand; a full turn requires another view. Strong action needs anticipation, acceleration, clear contact and recovery. Grounded hips/feet and arm/prop paths must remain consistent even when the edit changes angle.

Cloth/hair follows the body with pinned roots and delayed response. Lighting and shadows remain attached to form and location. Motion blur, dramatic crops and speed-line graphics cannot conceal a missing pose or disconnected grip.

## Camera and edit grammar

Move between place, person and relationship. A wide landscape can establish geography, a close-up can expose a decision, and a changed viewpoint can show its consequence. Rhythmic montage is valid when it creates a clear emotional or causal progression; the source's compilation format is not permission for an arbitrary slideshow of unrelated attractive images.

Use a measured hold when the viewer needs to observe a gesture or space. Use a shorter shot when its information is immediately legible. A push-in narrows attention; a pullback reveals context or aftermath. Choose timing from the actual story and complete narration. There is no universal slow cadence or fixed drawing frame rate for this family.

Cut on eyeline, geography, movement or meaningful contrast. Preserve direction and location in action sequences. A close-up montage still requires new performed states between images. A collage composition is optional and needs a reason; do not copy the source edit's exact layout.

## Environmental motion and entrances

Use depth-proportional parallax with the distant horizon moving least. Branch roots stay fixed while leaf clusters rotate/lag locally. Clouds drift coherently. Water ripples remain inside the water plane and follow perspective; reflections align with the surface and reflected subject. One image-wide distortion bends the world incorrectly.

Let environment respond to one lighting/weather system. A wind change can foreshadow danger or a sunbreak mark resolution, but the narrative also needs a visible character change. Mist belongs to an explicit depth layer with controlled entry and occlusion. A roof, wall or foreground tree can reveal a character as the camera moves, with sufficient clean coverage behind it.

Do not claim unseen geometry from a single frame. Modest layered movement is feasible; larger camera changes require new assets or geometry. Background parallax alone cannot match full painted-cel acting.

## Narration, sound and captions

Provider choice follows [narration setup](../narration.md): ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or none. Style direction never overrides that choice.

Keep the complete approved script and measure its take before deciding the shot budget. If dramatic action/observation cannot fit, follow [script and duration](../script-duration.md): reuse existing authorization or ask the current user before extending or changing the script. Never silently cut story to fit a preset length.

Use the chosen narration provider or supplied take under [narration choices](../narration.md), and local Python synthesis for original tonal score, air/water-like ambience and stylized foley. Give footsteps, cloth and weapon sounds appropriate material/perspective. More complex naturalistic foley may need another authorized source. Transition ambience with the location; music should follow tension/release and leave speech clear. No reference soundtrack recreation is implied.

Caption actual spoken phrases away from eyes, skyline, hands and important environmental detail. Keep backing/readability consistent during bright/dark cuts without making the film look like a webpage.

## Representative preflight and failure cases

Select proofs for the mechanics actually required by the current brief. Do not add a character, fight, camera move or transformation merely to satisfy an example below; a simple nature shot may need only its own release/contact and material proof. Explicit requests for silence, no narration or no captions override the defaults above.

Prove a close-up performance or changed-view body action, plus the hardest environmental/camera move, using production assets. Inspect normal speed, adjacent contact/cut frames, facial identity, support, parallax, reflections and maximum crop. Review the actual encoded output, not only attractive stills.

Fail for a beautiful static background behind an unchanged paper-puppet rig, rubber face rotations, random montage, floating reflections, globally bending scenery or the same choreography/camera as another style. The parallax lab does not certify production cel acting. Apply [motion direction](../motion-direction.md), [physics and review](../physics-and-review.md), [motion recipes](../motion-recipes.md) and the [production standard](../production-standard.md).

**Adaptable example:** a close eye reaction leads to a wide rooftop view revealing what the character sees, then a drawn turn and a matched action cut establish their decision; a quieter landscape holds the consequence. Another story may need only gentle observation. Choose the grammar from the prompt, not from the source compilation's order.
