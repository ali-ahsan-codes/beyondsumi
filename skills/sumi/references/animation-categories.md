# The final Sumi catalogue

Authority: the user's 20 October 2026 three-style decision, extended on 21 October with the common image-sheet/crop method and selective dark red in Dark. Exactly three styles: **Sumi Original, Sumi Light, Sumi Dark**. This replaces the interim Sumi Colo/Dark decision and all earlier four/eight/nine menus.

| Style | Identity and animation | Workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Sumi Original | The original Sumi-e film's painted scene, articulated/weighted character motion, cloth/wind, atmospheric depth and cinematic cause-and-effect. Predominantly ink on warm paper; a restrained meaningful accent can remain. | [Preserved Original](styles/sumi-original.md), [image-first](image-first-workflow.md), [camera/acting](animation-density-and-camera.md). |
| Sumi Light | Colourful brush/ink movement: expressive carbon silhouettes, translucent selected mineral washes, visible internal action, depth and evolving pigment. | [Light](styles/sumi-light.md) and [shared technical workflow](brush-ink-workflow.md). |
| Sumi Dark | Dark stories: oversized splash-built forms, camera-led ink journeys, black/grey and selective dark red; shared checked source preparation, distinct presentation. | [Dark](styles/sumi-dark.md) and [shared technical workflow](brush-ink-workflow.md). |

Recommend **Original for richer painted character stories**, **Light for romance, children’s/general stories and documentaries**, and **Dark for horror/mystery/dark stories**, respecting explicit user choice. Light/Dark reuse source preparation and exposure helpers; their visual direction is distinct. Dark uses much larger brush/splash-built subjects and more camera-led layered movement, not Light with a black/red filter. Original retains its Sumi-e identity with action-driven sources and20 designed character states/s at30fps. All are YouTube films with composed animated openings/endings, storytelling, captions and Python sound designed together.

The user's current instruction always wins over historical documents. Preserve research evidence and rejected drafts; old positive labels do not make them accepted. Do not reinstall superseded packages or automatically map an old preference to a new style without explanation.
