# ALI Animation source

The primary product is ALI Animation Studio in `studio/`. Read `README.md` and `INSTALL.md` for its local-first workflow. The optional `skills/` tree contains separate legacy assistant and production integrations; do not treat them as Studio dependencies. Preserve release/version checks and existing animation/project contracts when changing them. Keep credentials, personal project memory, recordings, and generated working files outside the repository. Generated exports belong in the ignored `outputs/` folder unless a task explicitly requires a tracked deliverable. Reuse the canonical ALI Animation brand assets and existing compatible work.

## Commit attribution

Preserve the repository's configured Git author and published history. Do not invent or substitute a person's identity, email address, or co-author attribution. Credit contributors only when their authorship is verified and they consent to the attribution. Do not rewrite history solely to alter contributor statistics.
