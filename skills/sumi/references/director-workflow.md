# From a beginner's idea to an approved film

Follow [scene-first cinematic production](scene-first-workflow.md) before finished generation: integrated shot master, matching supported views, storyboard/animatic/layout, reusable assets, action-driven poses and actual sound events. Derive layers from coordinated compositions; intact contextual panels are valid when separation adds nothing.

## Understand without interrogating

Read the current request, user memory and existing project memory. Ask only missing questions that change the film, in one friendly batch of at most three questions. Useful groups are: (1) story/purpose, audience and desired feeling; (2) where it will play, shape and approximate duration; (3) narration/language and must-keep details or references. Offer “you decide” with a sensible recommendation. Do not ask the user to choose lenses, drawing cadence or technical settings.

Use [story writing](story-writing.md) for length/telling-mode intake, an engaging hook and emotion-led narration before scene design. If a story is absent, develop a short original story with a clear change and meaningful ending. If supplied, preserve its approved wording, displayed text and indispensable actions; propose improvements openly. Never call new writing “recovered narration.” A simple poem, nature study or abstract idea need not become a hero's journey. When the user says “you decide,” direct the details and present the plan; do not keep asking small creative questions.

## Recommend a style on every new brief

State “I recommend **[style / route]** because [specific connection between this story and its performance/camera/material vocabulary]. Available: Sumi Original, Sumi Light, Sumi Dark.” If the user chose a style, affirm that choice and explain the direction within it. The recommendation can evolve while writing, but do not switch an approved style without explaining and obtaining the changed choice. Show the one-image atlas on first use and when the user wants to compare again; do not flood every reply with it.

Use [animation-categories.md](animation-categories.md), [motion-direction.md](motion-direction.md) and character-integrity.md. Fill the category motion contract in the direction plan, including passage references, material ownership, inside-shot behavior and the intended motion proof. Direct acting, poses, tempo, framing, camera, transitions, atmosphere and sound from the story. Light and Dark share preparation/clock tools; Light emphasizes documentary clarity and story colours, while Dark uses broad splash-built forms and stronger camera-led drama. Original preserves its Sumi-e movement identity.

## Act as director

Apply [balanced drawings and story-directed camera](animation-density-and-camera.md). Budget the actual whole-pose changes under the selected style profile before production and align subject focus with the narration and emotional beat. A zoom is not an acting pose or a new viewpoint; add checked drawings when the action or angle requires them.

Choose scenes from the story's causal and emotional needs. Specify framing (wide/medium/close), viewpoint (eye/high/low), subject blocking, camera position and movement, push-in/pullback or zoom, transition and sound. Explain the purpose of significant choices: a high view can reveal vulnerability or geography, a low view can reveal scale, a close view can reveal a decision. None of these meanings is automatic. A static camera can be the right choice; do not force zooms, camera shake, cuts or effects into every scene.

For each shot record: time range; spoken words; required visible action; start/end state and consequence; staging and depth; pose assets; camera and focal target; transition into/out of the shot; music/ambience/effect cue; caption space; difficult contact or movement to prove. Maintain screen direction, eyelines, prop ownership, light and geography across edits. Show relationships through performed action, not just narrated claims.

Use the [direction-plan template](../assets/templates/DIRECTION-PLAN.md). The assistant fills it; the user should see a readable treatment and scene table rather than implementation jargon.

## Review, then request one concrete approval

Before generating paid assets/narration or producing the dependent animation, review the written plan for complete story, voice/visual redundancy, achievable action, style identity, content preservation and an ending with time to register. Present:

- Story or final proposed narration, with edits distinguished from approved text.
- Chosen style/route, reason and the other two alternatives.
- Scene/camera/transition direction, narration and sound approach.
- Estimated full duration/range, requested format and what remains an estimate.
- Any actual third-party install/model download still needed and any paid-generation estimate.

Ask approval of this specific revision and wait. “Looks good, make it” approves the presented plan; it does not authorize an unrelated subscription or hidden runtime change. Record approval with its date, message/source, plan revision and selected duration. Do not fabricate a prior approval. Once approved, continue within it without repeated questions about every shot.

Follow [script-duration.md](script-duration.md): if the complete film exceeds 30 seconds or another requested target, show the complete estimate and a concrete shorter alternative specifying changed words/beats. Let the user decide. Do not cut, rush speech, omit actions or extend on their behalf. An older broad “make it longer” is not a later exact-runtime choice when the user has asked to choose again.

Technical setup and non-paid research may continue while approval is pending; dependent paid production and full-film rendering wait. A narration-free film still needs a scene plan and duration approval. Supplied audio sets timing from the recording but does not remove story coverage review.

## Build, prove and deliver

First follow the shared scene-first workflow: review storyboard/rough animatic and layout, then compose complete contextual masters and planned whole action states before optional layer extraction. Follow [character integrity](character-integrity.md). The three-style contract and selected technical profile override conflicting legacy recipes.

After approval, prove the hardest category-defining passage and any difficult physical action (often one 3–6-second study) with provisional assets; recheck with final artwork. Use actual world-space support, grips, release momentum, collision, cloth/petals, depth and persistent outcomes. Review adjacent frames and normal-speed motion. A helper test or still atlas cannot certify a finished performance.

Build the full film with separate editable shots and voice/music/effect stems. Measure actual narration before locking timing. Follow [the shared title/caption/sound rules](hooks-endings-sound.md): retain concise story captions even without narration unless explicitly text-free. No narration means no voice calls/model downloads; music/ambience/effects lead without a narration duck. Read [dialogue casting and lip-sync](dialogue-and-lip-sync.md) only for speaking roles. Sound continues unless silence is requested.

Review the actual final revision for story coverage, distinct motion, sound/listening, captions, playback controls and encoded export using [production-standard.md](production-standard.md). Deliver the MP4, editable source, direction script, asset provenance and review status. Name unperformed checks; do not claim zero errors or call unfinished drafts final. Keep project memory current at meaningful milestones. Publishing is a separate user request.


## User-selected editions
Produce the edition the user selects. Do not generate all three by default. Delivery shows the complete catalogue, but unmade styles prepare a recreation request for the user to send to their current agent. This works independently of Codex, Claude, OpenClaw or another host. Clicking a static dashboard button does not itself authorize or launch hidden production. Preserve the approved project when recreating an explicitly requested edition.

For saved choices and supplied dialogue files, use [the new-project intake](new-project-intake.md); do not repeat first-run setup.
