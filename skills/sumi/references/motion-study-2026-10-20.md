## Method and limits

Compare separated moments and short consecutive intervals: starting form, change, consequence and transition. Native browser playback/scrubbing and screenshots supplied the evidence; no source video was downloaded, reused as artwork or fed into a video-generation service. Source audio was not analysed. Times below are approximate media times, not measured drawing cadences. A paused screenshot cannot by itself establish smoothness, and sampled passages are not a frame-by-frame review of each complete film. Toolchains, fluid accuracy and unseen production methods remain unknown unless separately credited/verified.

Some players initially returned poster frames at time zero; those failed seeks were excluded, playback activated and samples recaptured. A related 33-second clip was initially targeted beyond its duration; those invalid samples were excluded. The private evidence manifest retains requested and actual times rather than relabelling failed captures as motion. Successful temporal crops were visually inspected. Original research screenshots remain outside the reusable skill/package.

## Changes this evidence requires

- Flow gains Bristle **and Bloom** routes, with material performance inside shots. Wet boundaries, subject formation, transitions and final drying/settling each need direction.
- Wash retains kinetic spatial painting. Print and Noir deliberately inherit that fluid movement family rather than being forced into rigid or stepped animation.
- Grain includes visibly active dots/grain with a separately specified subtle screen-space layer, while keeping object-bound texture and both original routes.
- Mist uses substantial fog and soft focus with staged recognition; diagnostics check hidden mechanics without permanently clearing the final atmosphere.
- Scenic includes an honest choice of 2D acting, 2.5D planes or real 3D geometry/views for the shot. Calling a zoom “3D” fails.
- Sketch retains its own gesture/response and redraw character.

These decisions are implemented in [the category contract](animation-categories.md) and individual files. They are reusable direction, not evidence that the eight existing comparison films have been rebuilt.
