# Print

Digital/woodblock print imagery with stable ink contours, pigment plates, paper and engraved detail, animated through simple fluid Wash-family performance. Ukiyo-e/woodblock aesthetics are adjacent to Sumi-e wash; this is an intentional variation in the collection. Preserve environment, intimate acting and substantial action as separate routes inside the family.

## Binding movement identity

Read [the category contract](../animation-categories.md). The current Print treatment combines a **digital/woodblock print appearance with simple, elegant Wash-family animation**. Stable engraved contours and pigment plates describe the image; they do not require rigid acting, stepped limbs or page-turn transitions. Use continuous weight transfer, controlled gesture arcs, pose changes, attached cloth and motivated depth/camera movement. A quiet story can stay economical; an action story needs actual action drawings.

**Build recipe:** animate the body/plant/wave with suitable curves, poses or constrained rigs, then render a registered outline, flat/graded pigment and locally attached engraving for each state. Keep hard material rigid and soft forms flexible. Graphic clouds or waves can sweep through depth to connect shots; their engraved structure remains coherent. Gentle local colour wash is compatible, but dissolving every engraved line into wet pigment would drift toward Flow.

**Identity proof:** show one complete story-relevant gesture from preparation through response with final print detail. Inspect contour continuity, engraving at the largest crop and motion at normal speed. Flowing movement must survive with texture disabled; printed material must remain stable when restored. Reject compulsory choppy exposure, rotating an entire paper doll, jumping colour plates, or reuse of Wash's whole shot graph without Print-specific framing and restraint. Preserve the environmental, intimate and action routes below; choose only what this story requires.

## Materials and assets

Use a stable contour with selective drawing variation. Separate outline and colour when useful. Choose muted pigment roles from the story rather than fixed swatches. Printing wear and slight plate misregistration can be fixed material features, never frame-by-frame RGB glitches. Fabric patterns follow local garment motion.

Prepare clean architectural layers with explicit hinges for moving gates, articulated plant roots/tips, separate faces/hands/held subjects, independent props and all required pose views. Plan the largest crop and final line-width target. Thin engraving must survive H.264 compression. Fine texture does not excuse vague hands or changing weapon length.

Each film owns its shot plan, camera, pose sequence and event timing. Reuse utilities, not another variant's complete choreography under a new outline.

## Environmental route

Pin branch bases and distribute bending along branches; tips lag and leaves respond locally. Open petals around their roots or through matched drawings while retaining calyx attachment. At detachment, preserve position/velocity before gravity, drag and rotation. First collision changes flight into settling, and the petal remains present.

Bricks and metal retain rigid geometry while a gate rotates around its hinge. Curled clouds translate or deform as designed motifs rather than noisy liquid. Camera/view progression must have sufficient hidden-area coverage and depth. Do not distort the whole painting to imitate wind.

## Intimate acting route

Direct intention, approach, hesitation, touch, received response and settling. Separate head/neck, eyelids, mouth, forearm, hand and held subject. Use alternate angles and face drawings where stretching would alter identity. Allow a complete small gesture to read within held framing when that serves the relationship.

Maintain grip/support as either subject moves. Dedicated hand-contact assets and compensating arm motion may be necessary. A close-up reveals meaning; it must not conceal missing continuity. Match position and velocity at endpoints only when a loop is requested. Do not force a complete story to return to its initial state.

## Action route

Author contact and extreme poses before in-betweens. Preserve volume, contour hierarchy and screen direction. A giant wave, crouched body and close facial reaction can need different drawings and projection. Strong foreshortening requires alternate views or suitable geometry; scaling a flat limb is insufficient.

A climb has fixed grip, weight transfer, release and regrip. A strike has stance, torso/arm contribution, arc, contact and follow-through. A landing compresses and settles. Drawing holds or smears are optional authored choices; do not prescribe 12 fps or assert a source cadence from sampled frames.

Choose camera angles that reveal support and force. Cut on a movement, eyeline or spatial relationship and match incoming poses. Graphic speed forms can amplify a real action; they cannot replace the body's movement. Frame borders and cartouches are optional.

## Atmosphere and edit rhythm

Etched rain uses coherent direction and explicit depth/occlusion. A cloud, wave or foreground branch can motivate a transition; otherwise use a clean causally connected cut. Texture remains attached while the scene changes. Avoid universal paper flashes.

The three routes may coexist when needed: environment establishes change, intimate acting reveals a decision and action shows its consequence. Their proportions come from the actual story, not a mandatory three-part sequence.

## Narration, sound and captions

Provider choice follows [narration setup](../narration.md): ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or none. Style direction never overrides that choice.

Use the complete approved narration, measured before final editing. Resolve speech/action runtime conflict through [script and duration](../script-duration.md): reuse authorization or ask the current user before changing length/script. Never silently omit lines.

The selected provider or supplied recording provides requested speech; skip this when narration is declined. Python synthesis supplies original score and stylized plant rustle, wood movement, cloth, footsteps and weight-specific impacts. A kiss, blossom and wrestler must not share one whoosh. If required naturalistic detail exceeds synthesis, use another authorized source and record provenance. No source soundtrack match is claimed from muted visual research.

Caption the actual take in quiet safe space. A printed cartouche is optional and must not crowd hands or engraved action. Preserve voice intelligibility and silence around key responses.

## Representative preflight and failure cases

Select proofs for the mechanics actually required by the current brief. Do not add a character, fight, camera move or transformation merely to satisfy an example below; a simple nature shot may need only its own release/contact and material proof. Explicit requests for silence, no narration or no captions override the defaults above.

Prove the hardest needed subcase with production assets: opening/detaching flower, held-subject contact, or perspective-changing action. A mixed brief needs evidence for each substantial route. Inspect normal speed, adjacent contact/release frames, final-size contours and compressed output.

Fail for drifting hand/paw contact, rubber faces, sliding patterns, bending bricks, gate geometry distortion, changing body volume, floating petals or a quantized common gait. The branch lab does not certify human action. Apply [motion direction](../motion-direction.md), [physics and review](../physics-and-review.md), [motion recipes](../motion-recipes.md) and the [production standard](../production-standard.md).

**Adaptable example:** a gardener steadies a damaged flower and closes a gate against the wind, using environmental and intimate routes. An athletic rescue needs an additional pose/view budget. Do not add loops or speed lines merely because another Print reference uses them.
