# Choose narration independently of sound

Offer **ElevenLabs**, **Voicebox**, **record your own voice and send it**, with **no narration** as a separate option. Reuse the chosen route and actual available connection/model/profile; a project-only cast does not replace a saved global default. A failed voice clone does not authorize switching voices.

- **ElevenLabs:** use the suite's `sumi-elevenlabs` companion when selected. Its setup guide owns secure credentials, current voice/model/endpoint permissions, character-based estimates and paid-take approval. Prefer an available connector; the independent helper is also at `assets/remotion-starter/scripts/elevenlabs_voice.py`, copied into new films. Read preferences/read-only capabilities before generation. Do not load both copies or duplicate calls.
- **Voicebox:** use the suite's `sumi-voicebox` companion. Its setup guide owns official OS requirements, selected model download/storage/language, presets/profiles and local API/version checks. Existing compatible app/models are reused; no implicit downloads. Voicebox is not an assumed music/foley generator.
- **User recording:** follow [supplied narration](supplied-narration.md). Full spoken story is used directly, with untouched source, actual duration/emotion/words and approved changes only. A voice sample is a different purpose; do not silently clone or upload it.
- **None:** skip voice credentials, models, calls and invented speech. Continue the story with music/ambience/effects and concise story captions under [the shared presentation rules](hooks-endings-sound.md).

Story writing and understanding use the chosen assistant; spoken language support is a separate property of the selected voice model/engine. Confirm the requested language and script rather than promising one language count for every route. Never replace Urdu with Hindi without asking.

For roles, read [character dialogue and lip-sync](dialogue-and-lip-sync.md). Save only explicitly selected non-secret route/voice/model IDs outside the package. Setup, possession of a key and reusable preferences do not authorize a new paid take. Inspect existing receipts/history before retrying an uncertain job. Actual pronunciation, complete speech and emotional delivery need review; tags alone prove none of them.

If a companion is not auto-discovered, read its sibling `SKILL.md` directly from this extracted suite. Do not invent host-specific install paths. Provider/model licensing, rates and features can change; use official docs and live account/app capabilities rather than frozen universal promises. Personal voice advice is optional and carries no monetization guarantee.
