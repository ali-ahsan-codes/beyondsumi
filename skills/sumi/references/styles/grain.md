# Grain

Graphic painted shapes with granular shading, purposeful scale changes and designed transformations. Preserve two routes: Tencent's graphic scale/motif construction and the Far East Festival's looser contours and sweeping forms. Sharing a material family must not erase their movement differences.

## Binding texture and movement identity

Read [the category contract](../animation-categories.md). Grain combines **visible granular/dot texture with Japanese ink/print sensibility and graphic performance**. Its aged-TV feeling comes from controlled grain, stipple and modest tonal flutter, not an obligatory television border, scanline glitch or snowstorm. Preserve the Tencent mass/scale route and Festival contour/sweep route. Use Japanese compositional/material cues appropriate to the story; do not paste decorative kanji, torii or samurai into unrelated subjects.

Separate two texture systems: **object-bound grain** gives painted mass and shading; an optional **restrained screen-space fine-grain layer** supplies the old-TV surface activity requested for this category. The latter is deliberate, not accidental texture swimming. Seed it by frame index or a cached short sequence so seeking is reproducible. Vary grain softly; do not flicker the entire luminance range. Large readable dots/stipple must survive the delivery size and compression. Avoid moiré from tiny repeating halftone grids; adapt spacing/filtering to the final pixel size.

**Build recipe:** first animate designed silhouette extremes, scale and contour correspondence. Mask stable stipple into local form; add directional brush marks with that form. Add the separate subdued grain layer only after the graphic action reads. In a transformation, grain moves with or redistributes through the transforming mass rather than merely dissolving between two posters.

**Identity proof:** examine a full graphic gesture/scale change and final-size texture movement. Compare texture enabled/disabled, and inspect both paused frames and normal-speed encoded output. Reject invisible grain, random anatomy boil, intrusive television glitches, or an ordinary walk scene whose only distinction is noise. The motion should communicate force or compositional transformation even before the texture pass.

## Materials and assets

Separate a clean silhouette from its tonal/grain surface. Large angular or curved masses carry composition; stipple gives substance. Keep edge bristles and brush direction selective, leaving clean regions. Choose palette relationships from the story; restrained tones and larger coloured fields are both possible.

Plan source contour, destination contour and a readable intermediate before generating artwork. Hands, eyes and creatures changing view need key extremes and alternate views. Normalize path topology/order for a true morph and author correspondence. For incompatible artwork, use a purposeful occluder or drawn smear. At the midpoint, show one comprehensible idea rather than ghosted pictures.

Keep grain in object coordinates using masked local surfaces. Threshold seeded noise against a smooth value gradient; cache it and inspect compression/zoom. Object-bound stipple must not alias or accidentally crawl. The separately authored screen-space old-TV grain specified above may move subtly without breaking object registration. Shared rendering helpers are useful; a shared complete shot graph and choreography with the other styles is not.

## Tencent route: gesture, scale and motif

Choose the dominant graphic idea and build a performed change around it. A small body pushing against a large mass needs braced support, bent joints, resistance and release. A hand or eye close-up needs a genuinely prepared drawing and articulation, not an enlarged generic head.

Scale travel can connect a tiny figure to its bodily effort, or move into a curved graphic landscape. Decide what is revealed and preserve a readable directional anchor. Use new views/geometry where projection changes. Do not pretend that a single flat image can provide a true spatial plunge.

A continuous curve, disk or travelling mass can link scenes if its correspondence survives the transition. Hold the resulting idea long enough to understand. Authored drawing holds may contrast with smooth camera/compositing if appropriate, but they are choices for the new film, not an asserted reference frame rate.

## Festival route: contour and sweeping gesture

Allow looser drawn contours and broad silhouette movement. A leading head/limb can initiate a sweep while its body/tail follows with delay. Parallel strands can flow across a field with coherent local spacing and direction, and a foreground mass can take over the frame to bridge a major scale change. Large colour fields become compositional events. Keep stable anatomical landmarks underneath elastic outer shapes; looseness is not random volume change.

Stage smaller gestures in open space before a broad form reorganizes it. A camera may follow the sweep and settle on the resulting relationship; the gesture itself can motivate a transition. Repeating one generic wipe after every shot loses causality.

Choose the route from the prompt and document why. They need not both occur in every Grain film. A hybrid needs a shared motif and coherent performance, not alternating palettes.

## Atmosphere, space and detail

Use designed cloud/landscape shapes for space and occlusion. Entrances and contour travel reinforce hierarchy; do not import the same horizontal haze from Wash. Maintain clean negative space around expressive shapes. If mist is useful, state its depth and narrative role.

Symbolic figures still require weight, support, consistent prop length and contact. A moving shadow can foreshadow a threat, but a consequential physical event must eventually be visible. Released pieces inherit motion and settle; avoid unrelated confetti.

## Narration, sound and captions

Provider choice follows [narration setup](../narration.md): ElevenLabs, Voicebox, supplied audio or none. Style direction never overrides that choice.

Preserve all approved lines and causal beats. Measure the complete take and leave time for scale reveals and their meaning. Resolve runtime conflicts under [script and duration](../script-duration.md): reuse existing authorization or ask the current user before changing length or script. Never silently cut narration.

Use the selected narration route and local Python synthesis for original score, granular pulses, contour accents and material-specific contact. Tencent can use compact precise phrases; Festival can allow longer sweeps and open musical phrasing. These are proposed treatments, not verified source sound. Pauses are as useful as accents; changing only a root note/filter is insufficient differentiation.

Caption the actual spoken phrases in a planned safe region. Avoid duplicate narration headlines. Do not let an extreme close-up or advancing graphic mass cover the text.

## Representative preflight and failure cases

Select proofs for the mechanics actually required by the current brief. Do not add a character, fight, camera move or transformation merely to satisfy an example below; a simple nature shot may need only its own release/contact and material proof. Explicit requests for silence, no narration or no captions override the defaults above.

Prove one difficult scale/contour transition and one expressive subject action using production assets for the selected route. Inspect start, midpoint, completion and normal-speed motion. Check grain stability, gesture extremes, support, prop contact and the meaning of the incoming shot.

Fail for ghosted crossfades, texture-only transformations, random stipple, unreadable silhouettes or another style's exact choreography/camera. The lab validates a local mechanism, not the complete reference films. Apply [motion direction](../motion-direction.md), [physics and review](../physics-and-review.md), [motion recipes](../motion-recipes.md) and the [production standard](../production-standard.md).

**Adaptable example:** an inventor's circular sketch becomes a wheel through a clear contour match, with a close hand action showing the effort that makes it move. A celebratory story might instead use Festival's sweeping group gesture. Invent a new motif and performance for each prompt.
