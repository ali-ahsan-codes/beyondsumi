Characters and backgrounds share irregular ink masses, translucent colour and paper, with readable silhouettes and geography.

Build complete contextual compositions before deriving independent layers. Use broad brush shapes with purposeful dry gaps/pooling rather than uniform outlined cartoons with decorative splatter. Keep distinct foreground/middle/distant wash layers and enough blank space for compact backed captions. Plan meaningful wide/detail views before drawing them.

Keep visible environments—clouds, water, light, foliage or rain—linked to the setting and unobstructed by effects.
