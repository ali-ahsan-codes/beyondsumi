# Images and geometry first, then directed animation

Follow [scene-first cinematic production](scene-first-workflow.md) before finished generation: integrated shot master, matching supported views, storyboard/animatic/layout, reusable assets, action-driven poses and actual sound events. Derive layers from coordinated compositions; intact contextual panels are valid when separation adds nothing.

Current production authority: Original30fps/20 designed character states per animated second, with action-driven sources and supported derived motion; [Light/Dark 24fps with character animation on twos](brush-ink-workflow.md). Older numerical drawing caps/cadences below are historical guidance when they conflict. Plan enough checked drawing states for the actual action and keep source count separate from exposure count.

Use this workflow in production and Teach Mode. The final catalogue is **Sumi Original, Sumi Light and Sumi Dark**; read [the style contract](animation-categories.md) and the chosen style guide.

1. Read the brief, approved script, runtime choice and direction. A new brief gets a recommendation and the three choices. Writing/reorganizing the skill does not start production or testing.
2. For all three styles use the [complete-pose sheet and crop method](action-pose-sheets.md), preserving the selected style's own artwork. Save complete character masters, supported views and whole action poses under [character integrity](character-integrity.md). Procedural characters also need inspectable complete designs and support anchors. Do not assemble unrelated generated limb fragments.
3. Choose the selected style's preparation:

| Style | Prepare | Animate |
|---|---|---|
| Original | Original Sumi-e design, complete characters/rig or checked poses, scene depth, expressive brush geometry and suitable separate props | 30fps with action-driven sources and variable exposures; matching complete poses, grounded action, cloth/wind, atmosphere, camera and consequences, informed by the first film without inheriting its defects. |
| Light | Wet-brush/splash-built complete masters and action states matching the background, shot-specific source allowance and mineral palette | 24fps output, character poses held on twos; independent depth, weather and ink. |
| Dark | Same source and motion preparation as Light; neutral value design with selective dark red | Black/white/grey brush pigment with dark red reserved for blood, eyes and meaningful character/scene details. |

4. Record source count, registered whole-pose geometry, support/grip/prop ownership, depth, exposure times and narration-linked camera cues. A still cannot reveal an unseen side or hidden limb. Multiply needs body occlusion, not just a white background.
5. Follow [the shared brush specification](brush-ink-workflow.md) for Light/Dark and [balanced camera/acting](animation-density-and-camera.md) for all styles. Use the older brush, wash, line, graphic and atmospheric mechanics as useful techniques inside the chosen style, never additional primary choices.
6. Once production/testing is authorized, prove the hardest bounded passage before a full film. Inspect real adjacent frames and normal-speed motion, including final transformed contact and anatomy. A fixed-camera monochrome diagnostic can expose missing movement; it is not a restriction on the finished camera or a claim that Light/Dark require unrelated engines.
7. Use the standard asset/proof gate and [style-profile schema](style-profile-schema.md). Current hashes and review records are required for full rendering. Metadata does not certify pixels. Finish with story, listening, captions, runtime, controls and export review under the production standard.

## Economy without a slideshow

Count source files, distinct drawings/regions, timeline exposures, simulation updates and encoded frames separately. A ten-pose atlas is ten sources for budget purposes. Reused instances, cached fields and derived render passes do not create new artwork. Do not call lowerfps a measured token saving.

For Original, additional whole drawings are useful where actual actions need them. For Light/Dark, use shot-specific source budgets sufficient for the action; the old two-pose/four-source cap is superseded. Use the [wet-brush character material contract](brush-character-prompts.md). Do not force anatomy or omit necessary poses to meet an obsolete cap.

Teach Mode must use the same actual implementation and selected profile as the clean film. Show only existing diagnostics and keep them out of the exported picture. Historical labs are preserved experiments, not proof of the new styles' finished quality.
